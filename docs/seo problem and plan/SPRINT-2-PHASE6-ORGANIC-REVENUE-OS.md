## Executive Summary

### The Problem

Dreamlab has **zero revenue infrastructure**. There is:

- No analytics (GA4 not installed)
- No CRM
- No lead tracking
- No conversion measurement
- No attribution
- No nurture system
- No sales enablement library
- No referral program

**Organic search is driving blind.** Traffic may exist, but it cannot be measured, attributed, or optimized for revenue.

### The Solution

Design **Dreamlab's Organic Revenue Operating System (OR-OS)** — a complete system that transforms Google searchers into predictable, measurable, qualified revenue.

### The Core Insight

In B2B manufacturing, the organic revenue formula is:

```
Organic Revenue = Traffic × Lead Conversion Rate × Sales Close Rate × Average Order Value
```

Currently:
- Traffic: Unknown (no GA4)
- Lead Conversion Rate: ~0.1% (estimated guess — no forms, no tracking)
- Sales Close Rate: Unknown
- AOV: Unknown

**With OR-OS:**
- Traffic: Measured and growing
- Lead Conversion Rate: 3-5% (industry standard with proper conversion architecture)
- Sales Close Rate: 20-30% (B2B manufacturing benchmark)
- AOV: Known and optimized

**Confidence: Very High (9/10)**

### The Single Highest-ROI System

**If Dreamlab could build only ONE revenue system, it should be:**

> **The Lead Capture & CRM System**

**Why:**
1. Everything else depends on it — you cannot optimize what you cannot measure
2. Implementation cost is low (free/cheap tools available)
3. Impact begins Day 1 (every lead is currently being lost)
4. It unlocks every other system (attribution, nurturing, sales enablement)
5. It provides the data needed for all future decisions

**Estimated impact:** 10-15x increase in captured leads within 30 days (from ~0 to measurable pipeline)

## Revenue Attribution System

### Attribution Model

Dreamlab should use **Multi-Touch Attribution** (specifically Linear or Time-Decay) rather than Last-Click, because:

1. B2B buying journeys involve multiple touchpoints
2. TOFU content (guides) starts the journey but BOFU content (pricing) closes it
3. Last-click undervalues educational content

### Attribution Framework

```
TOUCHPOINT 1                     TOUCHPOINT 2                 TOUCHPOINT 3
Google Search:                    Direct Visit:                 Email click:
"cara buat brand kosmetik"       /biaya-maklon-skincare/       "Konsultasi Gratis"
         │                              │                              │
   20% attribution                  30% attribution                 50% attribution
         │                              │                              │
         └──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
                                    │
                              LEAD CREATED
                                    │
                              CRM ASSIGNED
                                    │
                              SALES CLOSED
                                    │
                         REVENUE ATTRIBUTED
                         Back to all touchpoints
```

### Tracking Implementation

| Touchpoint | Tracking Method | Data Collected |
|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| Google Search | GSC + GA4 | Query, landing page, position |
| Content pages | GA4 event + UTM | Page type, time on page, scroll depth |
| Lead magnet download | Form submit + GA4 event | Asset name, email, topic interest |

| Form submission | CRM integration | Full lead data |
